# Army Channel — TEST BATCH Pack (5 Shorts)

**Status:** Test batch only. Nothing has been uploaded or posted anywhere, and no channel has been created. These are for preview and a go/no-go decision.

**Production spec (all 5):** Vertical 1080×1920, H.264 + AAC, fast cuts (~6s beats, strongest visual first), bold English hook text burned in for the first ~3.4 seconds, light cinematic grade. **No source audio is used** (several sources had narration/music of their own — all stripped). **Music:** three original tension beds synthesized for this batch (dark drone + pulsing kick + riser, loudness-normalized to −14 LUFS) — no copyrighted tracks, no library tracks, so there is nothing to claim. Tone is neutral and factual throughout: no Pakistan-vs-India content, no combat footage, no casualties — training, ceremony and hardware only.

## Short 1 — Tomb Guard Precision
**File:** `army-short-1-tomb-guard-precision.mp4` · **Duration:** ~36s
**On-screen hook:** "21 STEPS. / 21 SECONDS. / TOMB OF THE UNKNOWN SOLDIER"

**Title options:**
1. 21 Steps. 21 Seconds. Zero Mistakes.
2. The Most Disciplined Soldiers on Earth?

**Description:**
The Tomb Guards at Arlington National Cemetery march exactly 21 steps, then face the Tomb for exactly 21 seconds — a tribute to the 21-gun salute, the highest military honor. Before taking post, every sentinel's rifle is inspected down to the last detail. This precision never stops: the Tomb has been guarded 24/7, in all weather, since 1937.

## Short 3 — SEAL Training
**File:** `army-short-3-seal-training.mp4` · **Duration:** ~36s
**On-screen hook:** "5.5 DAYS. / 4 HOURS OF SLEEP. / U.S. NAVY SEAL TRAINING"

**Title options:**
1. 5.5 Days. 4 Hours of Sleep. SEAL Hell Week

**Description:**
Hell Week is the defining test of U.S. Navy SEAL training (BUD/S): five and a half days of near-constant physical training on roughly four hours of sleep in total. Only about one in four candidates makes it through SEAL training. The footage shows SEAL Qualification Training and demolition training — the stages that turn candidates into SEALs.
